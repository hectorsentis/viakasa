# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Spain and international buyers who need to buy, sell, value or manage a home in Madrid, Tenerife or elsewhere in Spain. They are making a high-stakes decision and need one reliable adviser who can coordinate the property, legal and financial work.

## Product Purpose

Viakasa presents a full-service property advisory and turns interest into qualified enquiries. Success means visitors quickly understand the firm’s expertise, find relevant properties, and feel confident requesting a valuation, viewing or consultation in Spanish or English.

## Brand Personality

Assured, warm and rigorous. The voice is direct and plainspoken, addresses the reader personally, and builds confidence without pressure or inflated claims.

## Anti-references

Not a high-volume property portal, a cold corporate consultancy or a flashy luxury broker. Avoid generic listing clutter, hard-sell language, excessive decoration, anonymous stock-team photography and interactions that obscure the next step.

## Design Principles

- Make trust visible through clarity, consistency and specific expertise.
- Keep one knowledgeable point of contact at the centre of the story.
- Let strong property and architectural photography carry the emotional weight.
- Make every primary action concrete and low-friction.
- Give Spanish and English visitors an equivalent, complete experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-operable navigation and forms, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, meaningful alternative text, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support where motion is introduced. Language changes must be explicit in navigation, document language and metadata.
